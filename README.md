## About

I build experimental products at the intersection of **AI, Web3, games, and developer tooling**.

My work usually focuses on practical user-facing prototypes: miniapps, on-chain interaction flows, AI-assisted interfaces, local automation tools, and game systems that can be shipped, tested, and improved in public.

- Based in Indonesia
- Working mostly with TypeScript, React, Next.js, Solidity, Python, and local AI tooling
- Interested in Farcaster miniapps, Base, autonomous agents, MCP workflows, ComfyUI pipelines, and game UX
- Building in public under the `0xheycat` handle

## Working Style

I prefer shipping small, real, testable iterations over polished mockups. Most of my projects are built around live integrations, practical UX, and reusable infrastructure that can survive beyond a demo.

I care about clean product direction, minimal fake data, real wallet flows, mobile-first UX, and tooling that helps builders move faster without hiding the actual engineering work.
